# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Implementer — Questions, Set 1
### Topic 1: Context of the Organization & ISMS Scope (Clause 4.1–4.4)

Attempt all questions before checking the answers file. Write down your reasoning, not just the letter — that's what actually gets tested in the exam's scenario questions.

---

**Q1.** A mid-sized fintech company is preparing its ISMS scope statement. It has three divisions: Payments (handles all customer financial data), Marketing (runs campaigns, no access to customer financial data, uses a separate SaaS CRM with its own security arrangements), and IT Infrastructure (manages network and servers for both other divisions). The CISO proposes excluding Marketing from the ISMS scope because "it doesn't touch sensitive data."

Is this exclusion justifiable, and why or why not?

---

**Q2.** During a Stage 1-style readiness review, you find that the organization has documented its ISMS scope but has not documented *which* interested party requirements (e.g., specific customer contract clauses, or GDPR obligations) will actually be addressed through the ISMS — only a general list of interested parties.

Which clause requirement is not being met, and what's missing specifically?

---

**Q3.** A company outsources its data centre operations entirely to a third-party cloud provider. It wants to scope the ISMS to cover only its own internal processes and exclude anything related to the cloud provider's infrastructure, on the grounds that "we don't operate the servers."

What does Clause 4.3 require the organization to consider here, and is the proposed scoping approach sound?

---

**Q4.** A retail company determines through its 4.1 analysis that new data protection legislation is coming into force in a market it operates in (external issue), and separately notes that it has a strong internal culture of information-sharing between departments that sometimes bypasses formal access requests (internal issue). How should these two findings influence the ISMS scope and later risk assessment work?

---

**Q5. (Multiple choice)** Which of the following is the MOST accurate statement about scope exclusions under ISO/IEC 27001:2022?

A. Exclusions are not permitted under the 2022 revision — the ISMS must always cover the whole legal entity
B. Exclusions are permitted only for business units with fewer than 50 employees
C. Exclusions are permitted, provided they are justified by the outcome of the 4.1–4.3 analysis and don't exclude something that shares interfaces/dependencies with in-scope activities
D. Exclusions are decided solely by the external certification body during Stage 1 audit
